FlockView

Poultry farm management system

FlockView is a poultry farm management system I built to help track and manage chicken barns and flocks. It includes tools for recording daily mortality, feed schedules, flock information, visitors, shipping, crop setup, and farm records. The goal is to make farm data easier to record, organize, and access in one place.

Technologies: HTML, CSS, PHP, MySQL, Docker, and Nginx.
